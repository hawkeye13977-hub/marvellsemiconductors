About SemiCore

Empowering engineers through semiconductor education.

Our Mission

SemiCore is an independent educational platform dedicated to semiconductor technology, SoC Design, RTL Design, ASIC development, AI hardware, and Design Verification.

Our goal is to provide practical tutorials, technical articles, interview preparation, and industry insights to help students and professionals grow their engineering skills.

This platform is independently developed and is not affiliated with any semiconductor company.

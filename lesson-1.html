Lesson 1

Introduction to RTL Design

Understand the foundation of digital hardware design and why RTL is the language of modern chips.

Learning Objectives

After completing this lesson you will be able to:

  • Understand what RTL means.
  • Explain why RTL is important.
  • Identify where RTL is used.
  • Understand the RTL development flow.
  • Understand ASIC vs FPGA development.
  • Read simple RTL examples.
  • Prepare for beginner interview questions.
  • Get ready for the next lesson.

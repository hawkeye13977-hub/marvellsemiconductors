Course Overview

Everything you need to become confident in RTL Design.

Register Transfer Level (RTL) Design is the foundation of modern digital hardware development. RTL describes how data moves between registers and how logical operations are performed during every clock cycle. RTL Design is widely used in:

  • ASIC Design
  • FPGA Development
  • SoC Architecture
  • AI Accelerators
  • Automotive Electronics
  • Networking Chips
  • Consumer Electronics

Career Opportunities

RTL Design is a core skill for semiconductor engineers across multiple domains.

💻 RTL Design Engineer

Design synthesizable RTL for ASICs, FPGAs, AI accelerators, networking and automotive chips.

✅ Design Verification Engineer

Verify RTL using SystemVerilog, UVM, assertions, coverage and simulation methodologies.
